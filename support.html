Frequently Asked Questions

My score or game progress is not updating, what should I do?

Please make sure your device is connected to an active internet connection, then try restarting the app.

How can I request data or account deletion?

You can request full deletion of your data at any time by sending an email to our support address with your account details.

How do I report a bug or glitch?

Please email us with a brief description of the issue, your device model, iOS version, and screenshots if available.
