Delete Your Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your account for Glowie - Neon Caterpillar Game.

How to request deletion

Send an email to: glowiegameapp@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your account.

Data that will be deleted

  • User account
  • Leaderboard information
  • Game progress associated with your account

Data that may be retained

Some records (such as security logs) may be retained for up to 30 days where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.
